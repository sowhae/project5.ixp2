my 2 crumbs

einstein's is kinda known for their “shmear,” which is basically their flavored cream cheeses, and honestly it surprised me in a good way. the strawberry shmear was way better than i expected… i thought i'd like the plain more, but the strawberry and honey almond actually stood out the most. i always enjoy a good asiago bagel and einstein's asiago did not disappoint. it's super easy and reliable, nothing crazy, but it hits when you just want a bagel. the price is pretty decent. only downside is they close at 2pm, so i had to lowkey rush. would i go again? yeah, probably the most out of all of theme especially for a quick, safe option.

7/10

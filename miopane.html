my 2 crumbs

it was pretty delish. my personal favorite was the everything bagel w/ smoked gouda — even better after a quick air-fry so the cheese melted perfectly. some of the bagels with garlic or onion were a bit much. would i go again? maybe if im really craving a savory bagel. however it is on the pricier side with each bagel being $7... i also go the matcha einspänner and it was DELISH. but there was a pretty long line. i waited for around 30 minutes. but i heard it can get up to an HOUR long... take that as you will.
